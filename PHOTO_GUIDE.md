## 🖼️ How to Use Your Own Real Photos:

1. Put your JPEG or PNG photos inside the `wishes` directory (for example `usha1.jpg`, `usha2.jpg`, `couple.jpg`).
2. Open the corresponding `.html` file in any text editor or IDE.
3. Find the `<img src="https://images.unsplash.com/..."` line.
4. Replace `https://images.unsplash.com/...` with your filename (e.g., `src="usha1.jpg"`).
